Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Is this really 100% free and open source?

Yes. SchemaStudio runs locally on your machine using Python, ffmpeg, and open-source models with free tier API rotational quotas.

How does the multi-key rotation work?

The system rotates through your ElevenLabs API keys dynamically. When one key is exhausted, it transparently falls back to the next key or Fish Audio.

What are timeline chapters on YouTube?

Because your channel is verified, timestamps starting at 00:00 automatically create interactive scrub bar chapter markers directly on YouTube's player.

How do I launch the production studio?

Simply click "Get Started Free" or "Launch Studio" at the top to access the full control dashboard with 1-click generation.
